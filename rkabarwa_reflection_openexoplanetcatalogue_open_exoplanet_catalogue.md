# Reflection: openexoplanetcatalogue_open_exoplanet_catalogue

Activity pattern: U-shaped. Longest gap: 2018-12 to 2019-05 (6 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 3. Current status: Active.

The Open Exoplanet Catalogue shows a U-shaped history: very high activity from 2011 to 2016 (1,733 commits in 2013), a decline to 51 commits in 2019, and a recovery to roughly 300 commits a year in 2023-2025. It has three gaps of three or more months; the longest is 2018-12 to 2019-05 (6 months).

Interpreting the gap required looking at who was committing. Almost all 2018 activity was catalogue data submitted as pull requests by one volunteer (ajtribick, PRs #1066 to #1086) and merged by the maintainer. When that volunteer paused after October 2018 there was simply nothing to merge. Recovery came from new volunteers in 2019 (a pull request in June, then a batch of NGTS, KELT, WASP and HATS additions in November), and later from automation: the most recent commits are all 'Automated nightly update from oec_continuity' by the maintainer's account. The project is active, but its activity is now a bot feeding data rather than a community of contributors.
