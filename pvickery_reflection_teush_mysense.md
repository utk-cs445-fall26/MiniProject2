# Reflection: teush_mysense

The project showed declining activity and had two significant gaps, including a 28-month gap from April 2022 to July 2024. The developer may have stopped making changes because the existing features were sufficient until new sensor services and APIs required updates. After the gap, work resumed on data services and station information. Recent 2026 commits mainly update presentations and participation documents, so the project is currently active even though much of its latest activity is not feature development.
