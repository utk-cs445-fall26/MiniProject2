# Reflection: epam_med3web

The project had irregular activity, including three gaps of at least three months. Its longest gap was from June to October 2024, and the cause is hard to identify from commit messages alone because the commits before it were mostly automated dependency updates. Developers may have been focused on other projects while automated maintenance continued. After the gap, contributors returned to improve features and fix issues. The most recent commits in 2026 involve visionOS wireframes and CI setup, and the project is currently active.
