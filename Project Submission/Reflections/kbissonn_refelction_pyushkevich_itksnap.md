# pyushkevich_itksnap Reflection
## Inactivity Patterns
- This project flips back and forth between super active and inactive with small gaps in-between. The overall activity follows a trend of being pretty active for a couple years, then stepping away for a while before going into another sprint. Very rarely does a true gap last more than a few months.

## Difficulty Interpreting Longest Gap
- The only things that was difficult about finding the longest gap was due to it being a lot of small gaps that are seperated by activity.

## Likely Reasons For Inactivity/Recovery
- These gaps are likely due to the developer trying to not burn out on the project. Another potensial reason for the jump in some of the slower years is the developer only fixing bugs or issues that are raised rather than developing new features.

## Activity Patterns
- Overall this activity pattern is irregular at a small scale, but becomes more cyclic when zooming out to see the entire project timeline as a whole.
