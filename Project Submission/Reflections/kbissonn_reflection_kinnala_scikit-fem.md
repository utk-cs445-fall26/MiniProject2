## Inactivity Patterns
- There are very few months where zero commits are made to the project, but there the project was slower towards the beginning and has started to slow down over the last three years with more months being inactive more recently.

## Difficulty Interpreting Longest Gap
- Due to the signifigant lack of gaps, it was really easy to find the longest one. However, the lack of gaps also meant that it is hard to confirm a pattern in the reasoning behind the gaps.

## Likely Reasons For Inactivity/Recovery
- The most likely reason for the inactivity is due to the developer changeing and needing time to get familiar with the codebase. Another potential cause is the project is just naturally coming to an end where only dependancy updates are being made as a lot of the bugs have been fleshed out and no more features are being added.
