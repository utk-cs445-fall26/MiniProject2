# Reflection: indralab_indra_db

This project had irregular activity with three significant gaps. The longest gap, from April to September 2022, is difficult to interpret because the commits do not say why development slowed. Developers may have prioritized other research or maintenance tasks. After the gap, contributors fixed bugs and worked on compatibility and data handling. The latest 2026 commits add biomedical knowledge graphs and AWS-related tests, so the project is active.
