# Reflection: ropensci_stplanr

The project had irregular activity and three significant gaps. Its longest gap was from February to July 2023. The exact cause is difficult to determine from the commits, but maintainers may have been busy with other responsibilities. Development resumed with bug fixes and improvements to routing and spatial functions. The latest commits in April 2025 focused on documentation, a routing parameter, and release preparation. Because there were no newer commits in the last 6 months, the project is currently inactive.
