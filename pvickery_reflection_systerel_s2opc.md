# Reflection: systerel_s2opc

The project had irregular activity, but only one significant gap, near the beginning of its history in 2016. The gap from April to June 2016 may have been caused by early planning before more implementation work. Development resumed with core functionality and fixes, followed by a large amount of later work. Recent 2026 commits involve testing, certificate handling, fuzzing, and queue improvements, showing that the project remains active.
