# Reflection: riatelab_cartography

Activity pattern: declining. Longest gap: 2024-02 to 2025-06 (17 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 6. Current status: Active.

cartography is an R package whose activity declined from a steady 60 to 140 commits per year (2015-2020) to almost nothing after 2021. It has six gaps of three or more months, and the longest runs from 2024-02 to 2025-06 (17 months), followed by a single commit.

The gap was easy to interpret. Every pre-gap commit by the maintainer is CRAN housekeeping: version bumps, NEWS updates, citation and URL fixes for a CRAN release. The only outside activity is two small pull requests in January 2024 and one issue in September 2024. The single post-gap commit (July 2025) is again a CRAN submission to keep the package on the archive. The riatelab team had moved its mapping development to the successor package mapsf, leaving cartography in maintenance-only mode. It has not recovered; the same maintainer keeps it installable, nothing more.
