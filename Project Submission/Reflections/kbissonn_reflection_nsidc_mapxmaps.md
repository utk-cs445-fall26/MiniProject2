- This project has super long periods of inactivity, then skyrockets before decreasing back down to a long period of inactivity.

## Difficulty Interpreting Longest Gap
- It is difficult determining what the longest gap was as there were so many. Then when it comes to actually looking at what caused the gap it is extremely hard to discern what the cause was.

## Likely Reasons For Inactivity/Recovery
- The reason for the longest gap was the project being on its way out and being finished off by an executive order, but the gaps before it typically fall into nothing needed to be added until there is some type of feature request that leads to a huge influx before settling back down again.
