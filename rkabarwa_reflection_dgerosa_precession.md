# Reflection: dgerosa_precession

Activity pattern: cyclical. Longest gap: 2018-12 to 2020-02 (15 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 7. Current status: Active.

precession is a scientific Python package whose commits arrive in cycles roughly every two to three years: 287 in 2016, 129 and 220 in 2020-2021, 295 in 2023, 69 in 2025, with near-zero years between. It has seven gaps of three or more months; the longest is 2018-12 to 2020-02 (15 months).

The gap was easy to interpret from commit messages alone. Before it, the author added Python 3 support and regenerated documentation; with version 1 complete there was nothing left to do. After it, the messages read 'storing precession v1 documentation', 'wiping for v2', 'setting up actions' and 'MIT license', a complete version 2 rewrite in May 2020 that produced the 2021 and 2023 bursts. The author drove the recovery, initially with one collaborator, and by 2025 new contributors are submitting pull requests that he merges. The project is active and its cyclical pattern matches release and paper milestones.
