# nanograv_enterprise Reflection
## Inactivity Patterns
- There are very few true gaps; however, the project has periods of higher commits and lower commits with some that will bottom out. 

## Difficulty Interpreting Longest Gap
- The hardest decision was debating to increase the threshhold to be more showing of the data as there are periods of time where the project only has a couple of commits for the span of months. However, I felt like leaving the initial threshhold is also symbolic of the data set as it is the start of another lull.

## Likely Reasons For Inactivity/Recovery
- The most likely reason for this inactivity and recovery is from the project only being worked on during certain times of the year and the developer from year to year being inconsistant, making it harder to determine whether the project is actually slowing down or new developers are just making bigger commits.

## Activity Patterns
- This project jumps from being pretty inactive for most of the year before jumping in activity, just to go right back down (ie. very cyclic).
