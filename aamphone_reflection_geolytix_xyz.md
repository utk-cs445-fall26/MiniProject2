# Reflection: geolytix_xyz

**Longest gap:** none · **Gaps of 3+ months:** 0 · **Pattern:** rising · **Status:** Active

## Activity pattern
This is the only project with no inactivity at all: every month from 2017-12 to 2025-11 has commits (15,306 commits from 65 authors in WoC). Volume grew from about 1,300–1,950 commits a year in 2018–2020, dipped to 756 in 2022, then climbed to 1,432 (2023), 3,340 (2024) and 3,539 (2025). The busiest single month was early 2025 (about 600 commits). The last point on the chart (2025-11) is low only because WoC's data stops on 2025-11-04.

## Interpretation
There was no gap to interpret, so Part 3's before/after themes are N/A. The pattern fits a company-backed open-source product: the repository belongs to GEOLYTIX, and a small core team (e.g., Dennis Bauszus and Rob Hurst in the latest commits) ships releases almost weekly. There were 44 tagged releases in 2025, and v5.0.0 followed in 2026-08. The 2022 dip lines up with the v4.0.0 major release (2022-11), after which activity accelerated.

## Recent themes
The 10 latest commits (late September to early October 2026) are mostly bug fixes (input guards, avoiding unnecessary queries, releasing database clients) around the v5.0.6 release notes and version bump. That is a mature, actively maintained codebase.
