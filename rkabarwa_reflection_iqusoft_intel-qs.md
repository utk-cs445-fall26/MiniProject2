# Reflection: iqusoft_intel-qs

Activity pattern: irregular. Longest gap: 2018-08 to 2019-01 (6 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 3. Current status: Active.

Intel-QS shows an irregular pattern: 1 to 13 commits a year in 2017-2019, a single burst of 299 in 2020 and 124 in 2021, then 16 to 29 a year. It has three gaps of three or more months, the longest from 2018-08 to 2019-01 (6 months).

The gap was moderately easy to interpret once the post-gap merges were read. Before it, the team added BigMPI support and began a naming-convention refactor; the first commits after it merge a 'consistent-naming feature branch' with non-backwards-compatible API changes. The work evidently continued on a branch that was not pushed, consistent with a small corporate team developing privately, and no issues or pull requests were opened in the period. The merge in February 2019, a bug fix in July and a CMake build-system rewrite in December set up the 2020 burst. The same Intel developers drove the recovery with one new contributor. The project is still marked active, but its 2025 commits are almost entirely dependabot dependency bumps, so real development has largely stopped.
